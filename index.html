What We Do

What We Do

Full Metal Productions is a full-service agency dedicated to high-impact, cinematic game trailer production. We partner with game studios to bring their worlds to life with maximum visual power, owning the entire creative process from concepting and scripting to advanced post-production and immersive sound design. We are pipeline polyglots, fully fluent in Maya, Unreal Engine, and hybrid environments, and by mastering these workflows we keep premium production fast and cost-effective for studio budgets without ever sacrificing quality.

Who We Are

A Cinematic Eye on an Engineered Pipeline

We started in 2019 as a group of gamers and 3D artists, and that has not really changed. FPS, mobile, MMO, hyper-casual: if it shipped, someone here has played it. Knowing the game is usually why we know what its trailer needs to do.

Full Metal Productions is a gaming agency fluent in the future of digital production, blending Unreal Engine, Maya, AI, and traditional and hybrid pipelines to deliver high-impact cinematic trailers and UA ads. We take fierce pride in our collaborations, and we are ready to scale your game's visibility.
